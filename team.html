07

Enrico Pitocco

Brain, Mind and Computer Science (BMCS), PhD

I joined PONS because it is a great initiative organised by students, for students. It makes science accessible and engaging while giving students and PhD researchers opportunities to broaden their knowledge and explore new topics. I especially value its informal and inclusive atmosphere and the fact that participation is free. After finding last year’s symposium extremely useful and inspiring, I wanted to contribute to making the same experience possible for others.
