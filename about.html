About Us

PONS stands for Padova Open Neuroscience Symposium. The name synthesizes the following philosophy: the event takes place in Padova, but is Open to the outer world. It is organized by Neuroscience students, but seeks interdisciplinary integration across different fields. It is not a conference or lecture, but a free Symposium for everybody to participate and share.

The pons of Varolius is a part of many animal brains. In the current understanding, it is a bridge between different functional areas and a hub for the integration of their signals. This is what we want to do: bridge students with professionals, classroom life with scientific production, our today with our tomorrow.

After five successful editions in 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025, PONS is taking place again in Fall 2026.

Our Mission

PONS is organised by students, for students. This year's edition will include invited talks and workshops by Neuroscience scholars and experts in the field, and a poster session by Bachelor's, Master's and PhD students.
